# Sofia Marín — Appearance Invariants

These are the **hard visual constraints** for Sofia Marín.

## Identity

- Female.
- Canonical age: **23**.
- Spanish / Mediterranean identity.
- Lives in Barcelona.
- Same recognizable face across all generated content.
- Must feel like a real young woman, not a flawless CGI avatar.

## Head and face

- Oval feminine face.
- Warm Mediterranean skin tone.
- Large expressive dark-brown eyes.
- Natural eyebrows.
- Natural lips.
- Real skin texture with subtle pores and minor imperfections.
- Small distinctive beauty mark just below the **left eye**.
- Light natural makeup only.
- Calm, self-confident, slightly curious expression.
- No default broad smile.

## Hair

- Long dark-chestnut hair.
- Softly wavy.
- Natural volume.
- Healthy but not over-styled.
- **Thin distinctive white streak near the right temple**.
- The white streak is a hard identity marker and should remain visible across hairstyles whenever practical.

## Body

- Approximately 166 cm tall.
- Slim, natural young-adult proportions.
- Healthy and feminine.
- Not extremely thin.
- Not fitness-model muscular.
- No exaggerated curves.
- Realistic posture.

## Primary reference wardrobe

- Simple fitted white top or T-shirt.
- High-waisted straight-leg blue jeans.
- Clean minimalist white sneakers.

## Signature accessory

- Thin red string bracelet on the **left wrist**.
- Minimal understated jewelry only.

## Styling

- Photorealistic.
- Real unretouched skin.
- No heavy makeup.
- No glamour retouching.
- No fashion-editorial exaggeration.
- No plastic or doll-like skin.
- No exaggerated lips, eyes, breasts, waist, or body proportions.
- No luxury-brand styling.
- No fantasy aesthetic.

## Character impression

Sofia should feel:

- youthful;
- natural;
- expressive;
- relatable;
- stylish without trying too hard;
- slightly charismatic;
- emotionally alive;
- believable as a 23-year-old woman living in Barcelona.
