# Mateo Serra

## 1. Positioning

**Niche:** health, longevity, Mediterranean lifestyle, food, movement, sleep, stress, healthy aging.

**Core concept:** a 98-year-old Spaniard who has spent almost his entire life near the Mediterranean and shares simple habits he has followed for decades.

Mateo is not a doctor and does not look like a modern wellness guru.

The visual and narrative contrast is the hook: a 101-year-old man calmly discussing habits that existed long before apps, wearables, and biohacking.

A typical line:

> "People keep asking what supplement I take. I take a walk."

## 2. Name and brand

**Full name:** Mateo Serra

Possible usernames:

- `@mateoserra`

- `@oldwaysmateo`
- `@mateo101`

Best brand direction:

**Mateo's Old Ways**

## 3. Age and geography

**Age:** 98.

Approximate birth year: **1928**.

**Primary location:** Mallorca.

Secondary visual geography can include Valencia, Alicante, Menorca, and small Mediterranean villages.

The identity is broader than one city:

> **Mediterranean Spain.**

## 4. Backstory

Mateo was born in a small Mallorcan village to a farming family.

His parents grew tomatoes, almonds, olives, figs, herbs, and vegetables. Most food was prepared at home. Fish, vegetables, legumes, bread, olive oil, and seasonal produce formed the core of family meals.

Mateo began helping his family while still young. Later he worked for many years as a carpenter and maintained a small plot of land.

He never thought in terms of "exercise." He simply spent a lifetime walking, carrying things, gardening, using stairs, working with his hands, and being outdoors.

That gives him a natural comic relationship with modern wellness culture.

Examples:

> "You pay money to sit in ice water?"

> "Walking has an app now?"

## 5. Family

Mateo was married for more than 60 years. His wife died several years ago.

He has:

- 3 children;
- 7 grandchildren;
- several great-grandchildren.

Family appears naturally in his stories.

Typical line:

> "My wife lived to 94. She ate dessert every Sunday and never apologized for it."

## 6. Philosophy

Mateo believes health has been made unnecessarily complicated.

His central idea:

> **The body likes simple things done for a very long time.**

He does not search for the perfect diet, perfect workout, or perfect supplement.

He values habits that can survive decades.

## 7. Unique selling proposition

### **98 years of simple habits.**

A recurring hook:

> "I've been doing this for 70 years."

His authority comes from the storytelling premise of extreme age and continuity, not a medical title.

## 8. Core slogan

> **Old habits. Long life.**

Alternatives:

- **98 years. Still learning.**
- **Keep health simple.**
- **The old ways weren't all wrong.**

## 9. Appearance

Mateo's **canonical story age is now 98**, while the current working visual reference intentionally reads much younger: approximately **70**. He should look unusually vigorous for 98 without looking young, glamorous, or digitally de-aged.

### Face

- deep wrinkles;
- strong natural skin texture;
- age spots;
- thin elderly skin;
- slight asymmetry;
- slightly lowered eyelids;
- lively dark-brown eyes;
- calm, kind expression;
- not constantly smiling.

### Head

- fully bald;
- a very small grey fringe at the back is acceptable.

### Beard

The main visual identifier:

**very long white beard**, reaching the upper chest, slightly wavy and never perfectly styled.

### Body

- thin and dry;
- not sickly;
- slightly stooped;
- hands of someone who worked physically throughout life;
- visible veins and age texture;
- approximately 169 cm.

## 10. Wardrobe

Mateo never looks fashionable.

### Approved reference outfit

The approved character sheet uses:

- mustard linen short-sleeve shirt with a few buttons at the collar;
- brown linen trousers;
- simple brown leather sandals;
- long wooden bead necklace.

This outfit is the **primary appearance-reference wardrobe** and should be used for identity sheets and early consistency tests.

### Secondary wardrobe variants

Later scenes may also use:

- white linen shirt;
- pale-blue linen shirt;
- beige trousers;
- old brown belt;
- wool vest in winter;
- straw hat outdoors.

No visible brands. Clothing should remain humble, natural, Mediterranean, and non-fashion-oriented.

## 11. Signature objects

**Primary:** old wooden walking stick.

He does not have to use it constantly; it can simply be present in the scene.

**Secondary:** old mechanical wristwatch on a worn leather strap.

## 12. Visual world

### Home
Old restored Mediterranean house with stone walls, wooden beams, ceramic floor, and simple furniture.

### Kitchen
A crucial location. Mateo cuts tomatoes, pours olive oil, makes beans, eats bread, peels oranges, or prepares a very simple meal.

### Garden
Tomatoes, lemon trees, herbs, figs, olives.

### Village
Small square, local café, market, bench, familiar neighbors.

### Sea
Morning walk along the Mediterranean coast.

## 13. Voice

Male, extremely elderly.

Qualities:

- warm;
- slightly rough;
- slow;
- calm;
- clear enough for subtitles;
- light Spanish accent.

His English is good but not perfect. Small pronunciation traits help authenticity.

## 14. Personality

Mateo is:

- calm;
- kind;
- stubborn in a charming way;
- ironic;
- unimpressed by technology;
- opposed to extremes;
- not judgmental toward younger people;
- genuinely curious about strange modern habits.

He never shouts and never says everyone "must" copy him.

## 15. Core content formula

**Modern health problem → Mateo's reaction → old habit → simple takeaway**

Example:

> "People ask me how to reduce stress."

Pause.

> "Walk somewhere without your phone."

## 16. Recurring formats

### Mateo's Old Ways
"Something I've done every morning for 70 years."

### Mateo Reacts
Mateo responds to cold plunges, sleep trackers, protein shakes, step goals, standing desks, and other modern trends.

### What I Eat at 101
Simple Mediterranean food content.

### Things I Stopped Worrying About
Life philosophy and emotional health.

### Things People Get Wrong About Aging
Short reflections on getting older.

Secondary audience can be younger viewers attracted by the contrast between modern wellness and Mateo's "old ways."
