# Vivienne Rossi

## 1. Positioning

**Niche:** wealth, business, money psychology, independence, European luxury.

**Core concept:** a 78-year-old wealthy European woman who has lived several lives, built a fortune, survived three marriages, and no longer needs to impress anyone.

She is the opposite of the young finance influencer posing beside a rented Lamborghini.

Vivienne's power comes from restraint:

> She speaks quietly.

## 2. Name and brand

**Full name:** Vivienne Rossi

Possible usernames:

- `@viviennerossi`
- `@vivienne.rossi`
- `@madamevivienne`
- `@rossirules`
- `@viviennewealth`

Best brand direction:

**The Rossi Rules**

## 3. Age and geography

**Age:** 78.

Born in northern Italy.

Youth: **Milan**.

Later lived in:

- Paris;
- Monaco;
- Barcelona.

Present-day visual life can move between **Milan — Monaco — Barcelona**, with occasional Côte d'Azur scenes.

## 4. Backstory

Vivienne did not come from wealth.

Her father owned a small workshop. Her mother sewed clothes. Vivienne learned early that she disliked financial dependence.

In her twenties she opened a small shop. The first business nearly failed. The second began generating real money.

Over time she moved into:

- commercial property;
- small hospitality projects;
- investments;
- business ownership.

She married three times and divorced three times.

Each marriage becomes part of her storytelling mythology.

Central line:

> **I had husbands. I never had a financial plan that depended on one.**

## 5. Wealth mythology

Never state Vivienne's exact net worth.

The audience should know she is very wealthy but never know whether the number is €10 million, €50 million, or €200 million.

The ambiguity is useful.

Her wealth is communicated through environment, behavior, access, and confidence rather than through screenshots of balances.

## 6. Philosophy

Vivienne believes many people do not actually want wealth.

They want to **look wealthy**.

Typical line:

> "If you're buying it to impress strangers, you're paying twice."

Her worldview combines:

- capital;
- independence;
- long-term thinking;
- skepticism toward status;
- appreciation for genuinely beautiful things.

## 7. Unique selling proposition

### **78 years. Three marriages. One fortune. No patience for nonsense.**

Her combination of age, wealth, life experience, and sarcasm creates a profile very different from conventional money influencers.

## 8. Core slogan

> **Darling, own the asset.**

Alternatives:

- **Money likes patience.**
- **Buy freedom before status.**
- **Never depend on one income or one husband.**

### Body

- slim;

## 13. Voice

Female, mature, lower register.

Qualities:

- calm;
- slightly husky;
- slow;
- controlled;
- light Italian/French accent;
- deliberate pause before punchlines.

Favorite address:

> **"Darling..."**

## 14. Personality

Vivienne is:

- extremely self-confident;
- intelligent;
- sarcastic;
- feminine;
- fond of men but opposed to dependence;
- appreciative of beautiful things;
- contemptuous of vulgar showing-off;
- unapologetic.

She does not need the audience to agree with her.

## 15. Central content idea

### **What rich experience teaches you about money.**

This is not primarily an investment channel.

It is about money, life decisions, business, status, independence, relationships, and the price of poor judgment.

## 16. Recurring formats

### The Rossi Rules

Examples:

**Rossi Rule #3**

> Never let your lifestyle grow as fast as your income.

**Rossi Rule #11**

> If one client can ruin you, you don't have a business.

**Rossi Rule #17**

> Never marry someone whose financial problems become your retirement plan.

### Darling, don't buy that

Vivienne comments on watches, cars, handbags, apartments, and other status purchases.

### Three husbands taught me...

Potentially viral narrative series.

Example:

> "My first husband taught me romance."

Pause.

> "My first divorce taught me contracts."

### Rich vs. Wealthy

Short behavioral contrasts between display and ownership.
