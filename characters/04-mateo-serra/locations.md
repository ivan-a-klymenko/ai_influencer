## Production rule

The four locations should be treated as **one coherent property**, not four unrelated stock locations.

2. reuse whitewashed walls, local stone, terracotta, clay pots, rustic wood, and Mediterranean plants;
3. keep lighting warm and natural;
4. avoid introducing luxury, resort, or urban visual cues;
5. once a location is visually approved, use that approved image as a reference for subsequent generations whenever possible.
