# Vivienne Rossi — Master Appearance Prompt

Status: **WORKING / CANONICAL**  
Selected on 2026-09-22 after visual review.

## Current character concept

- **Story age:** 78
- **Target visual age:** approximately 60
- **Core qualities:** exquisite refinement, elegance, poise, wealth, self-possession, European luxury

## Exact working prompt used for the approved image

```text
Create a photorealistic character sheet for Vivienne Rossi, a 78-year-old self-made wealthy European woman who visually looks around 60 years old.

Layout: two panels on a clean white studio background.
- Left panel: full-body standing view.
- Right panel: close chest-up portrait.

The same identical person must appear in both panels, with the same face, same apparent age, same hairstyle, and the same overall identity.

Vivienne Rossi should look like an exceptionally elegant, refined, wealthy European woman. She is chronologically 78, but visually she should read closer to about 60: very well preserved, poised, sophisticated, and striking, without looking young or artificial. She has a perfectly styled silver-white bob haircut, refined mature skin with natural texture, subtle elegant age lines, high cheekbones, sharp intelligent eyes, and a calm, self-assured, slightly severe expression. No smile, or only the faintest suggestion of a knowing expression. She should project unmistakable refinement, elegance, poise, taste, and high social status.

She has a slim body, excellent upright posture, graceful stance, and a naturally commanding presence. She should look polished, expensive, and powerful, not fragile.

Wardrobe:
- a cream tailored trouser suit or elegant cream fitted outfit,
- a white silk blouse or refined top underneath,
- understated elegant shoes such as refined heels or luxury loafers.

Signature accessories:
- oversized dark sunglasses, either worn or held naturally,
- a large gold ring with a green stone on the right hand,
- subtle expensive jewelry,
- optionally a silk scarf if it feels especially elegant and understated.

Style and realism:
- photorealistic
- real mature skin texture
- no heavy glamour retouching
- no cartoonish luxury styling
- no fantasy aesthetic
- no dramatic cinematic background
- no extra props unless minimal and natural
- emphasize sophistication, elegance, refinement, and high-end European taste
- the same identity in both panels

The overall impression should be: an authentic wealthy European luxury woman — self-made, exquisitely elegant, highly refined, poised, stylish, powerful, and visually unforgettable.
```
