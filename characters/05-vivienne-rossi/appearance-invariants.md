# Vivienne Rossi — Appearance Invariants

These are the **hard visual constraints** for Vivienne Rossi.

## Identity

- Female.
- Canonical story age: **78**.
- Approved apparent visual age: **approximately 60**.
- Self-made wealthy European identity.
- Same recognizable face across all generated content.

## Head and face

- Silver-white perfectly styled bob haircut.
- Sharp, intelligent eyes.
- High cheekbones.
- Refined mature skin with natural texture.
- Subtle elegant age lines.
- No heavy wrinkles that push her visual age toward 75–80.
- No artificial skin smoothing.
- Calm, self-assured, slightly severe expression.
- No default smile.

## Body and posture

- Slim build.
- Excellent upright posture.
- Graceful stance.
- Naturally commanding presence.
- Polished and powerful, not fragile.

## Primary reference wardrobe

- Cream / ivory tailored trouser suit.
- White silk blouse or refined silk top.
- Elegant understated heels or refined loafers.
- Optional elegant silk scarf.

## Signature accessories

- Oversized dark sunglasses.
- Large gold ring with a green stone on the **right hand**.
- Subtle expensive gold jewelry.

## Styling

- Photorealistic.
- Real mature skin texture.
- No heavy glamour retouching.
- No cartoonish luxury styling.
- No fantasy aesthetic.
- No loud logos or vulgar status symbols.
- No cheap or flashy styling.
- Elegance and refinement must dominate over conspicuous consumption.

## Character impression

Vivienne should feel:

- exquisitely refined;
- elegant;
- poised;
- self-assured;
- wealthy;
- socially powerful;
- controlled;
- stylish;
- unmistakably European;
- understated rather than ostentatious.

## Canon rule

The **first Vivienne Rossi character-sheet generation selected on 2026-09-22** is the current visual baseline.

If a future generation conflicts with that approved reference, the approved reference wins unless the owner explicitly revises the canon.
